# Seller business daily routine

Use this routine with the personal workspace built in SunsetPulse. Open `/today` after signing in and completing personal planner setup. Seller requests appear for active websites owned by your account.

## Start the day

1. Review the seller panel on Today: new requests, overdue actions, upcoming consultations, and recorded outcomes.
   If seller activity is unavailable, use **Retry seller activity** or open the seller inbox. Missing or invalid summaries do not mean there is no work. After scheduling from Today, the same read refreshes your requests, **Coming up**, and delivered reminders. A saved task appears on its due date; its reminder appears when the existing scheduler delivers it. If the schedule needs a refresh, use **Refresh schedule** or retry seller activity. A failed read preserves the previous schedule and its refresh notice; retrying does not create another task.
2. Open `/seller-inbox`. Read each request and its requested-contact permission. Browse older pages when needed; previous-page navigation preserves your path.
3. Open an eligible email draft, review it, and send through your email application. Use **Record email contact** after making contact. Expand **Record an earlier contact or reply** when entering work that happened earlier; times use the workspace timezone.
4. Schedule the initial response when it still needs handling. After a customer reply, record it and schedule the follow-up from that saved receipt. Confirmed consultations can be reopened through **Schedule confirmed consultation**, with the confirmed time preserved.

5. When that seller action already has a planner task, choose **Open saved planner task**. The dialog shows its saved status and opens its current due date, including completed work and dates in past years. If the schedule check fails, use **Retry schedule lookup** before creating a task; your draft stays intact.

## Handle due work

- In **Coming up**, check the task's due date and scheduled time in your workspace timezone. Use **Open planner task** to open that date, or **Open seller request** to review the linked request and record what actually happened. The planner also offers **Open seller request** for linked tasks. Request links disappear on a fresh read when the website is no longer active or owned by your account; your private planner task remains available.
- Use **Open scheduled action** on Today to open the task’s due-date planner, including dates in past years.
- In **Needs your attention**, use **Open reminder task** to open its due-date planner. **Dismiss reminder** hides that reminder; **Snooze 24 hours** schedules it for 24 hours after the first click. Neither action completes the planner task or changes its due date.
- If a reminder change cannot be confirmed, retry the same action. Its request and original snooze deadline stay intact. Use **Refresh reminders** to read the current state before switching actions; a failed refresh keeps the pending retry available.
- If a reminder reports that it changed, use **Refresh reminders** before acting again. Both action buttons stay paused through a failed refresh. A reminder already handled elsewhere disappears after the successful read.
- Complete a task when its work is finished. Record seller contact or a reply separately when that event actually occurred.
- If a save’s response is uncertain, retry the unchanged action. Refresh after a conflict before making another change.
- While editing a schedule, correct any date, time, or time-zone validation message before saving. Incomplete entries keep the draft open; correcting the time zone preserves the date, time, and reminder choice.
- If scheduling reports a conflict, use **Reload seller request** in the inbox dialog. Its date, time, and reminder draft stays intact while you reload. Review the refreshed request before saving; a withdrawn permission or unavailable request dismisses the draft. Today’s scheduling shortcut offers **Review current seller request** to open the inbox after a conflict.
- If the seller withdraws requested-contact permission, use **Revoke requested contact** and review the confirmation. Its linked pending seller tasks/reminders are cancelled; separate marketing permission remains unchanged.

## Close the day

1. Record confirmed consultations or closings in seller outcomes. Use the existing cancellation or closing-void controls to correct a record.
2. Open `/business` to record money actually received, expenses, and bill payments. A seller closing and its cash receipt are separate records. Keep expected income separate from received money.
3. Check the next appointments and follow-ups in `/planner`. Add campaign backlog work to the planner when it is ready to execute.

## Review the week

1. In `/planner`, choose the **Weekly business review** template and schedule the review.
2. Open **Review week** when it is due. Check upcoming dates, unrecorded expenses/payments, and the recorded seller counts and campaign groups.
3. If seller counts are unavailable, retry the read; the priority and next-action draft stays intact. Missing or malformed counts are unavailable rather than zero.
4. Choose one priority, one next action, and an optional friction note. Complete the review after checking its confirmations. Completion counts once per owner-local week.

## Choose the next improvement

After two weeks of actual use, review unanswered requests, overdue actions, consultations, recorded closings, and repeated friction notes. Pick the next product change from the handoff that cost the most time. Campaign request/outcome groups provide recorded evidence; they do not establish causation.
